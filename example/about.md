This is the demo site for **System Halted**, a Nord-palette Jekyll theme built for
writers and developers who want a fast, accessible blog without wiring up a pile of
services. Everything here runs as a static site: search is client-side (no server,
no third-party index), the light/dark toggle remembers your choice, and posts are
organized through a responsive card grid, categories, tags, and sortable archives.
SEO tags, a sitemap, and an RSS feed are generated for you.

The posts on this site double as documentation — each one walks through a feature of
the theme, from [search](/jekyll-theme-systemhalted/) to the
[Nord palette](/jekyll-theme-systemhalted/) to writing with Markdown and code.

**Making it yours:** edit `_config.yml` to set your title, tagline, author, and
sidebar navigation. Group your categories into themed sections by editing
`_data/taxonomy.yml`. Drop posts in `_posts/`, add `featured: true` to surface a post
on the Featured page, and you're publishing.
